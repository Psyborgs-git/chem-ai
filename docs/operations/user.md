# Studio usage

The current usage manual is organized by the work you are doing:

- [Getting started](../usage/getting-started.md): sign in and find your way around.
- [Projects and tasks](../usage/projects-and-tasks.md): research questions, task modes, and contracts.
- [Notebooks and editing](../usage/notebooks-and-editing.md): notes, recipes, formulae, revisions, and conflicts.
- [Research library](../usage/research-library.md): sources, Downloads, citations, and research memory.
- [Agent and providers](../usage/agent-and-providers.md): conversations, model choices, consent, and recurring work.
- [Materials and candidates](../usage/materials-and-candidates.md): registries and versioned candidate work.
- [Experiments and evidence](../usage/experiments-and-evidence.md): plans, manual measurements, review, and closeout.
- [Learning and models](../usage/learning-and-models.md): datasets, training, evaluation, and private models.
- [Simulations and calculations](../usage/simulations-and-calculations.md): computed results and their limits.

Open **Help & documentation** after signing in to read these manuals in the app.
For setup or backend failures, use [installation](installation.md) and
[troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) directly from the repository.
